About Vela

We built this because we
lived the problem.

Vela was built by a B2B marketing operator who ran demand gen at a growth-stage fintech company — and spent too many hours explaining his brand to AI tools that forgot everything the next day.

The story
Why we built a repository, not another content generator
AD

"I spent two years running demand gen for a fintech company as a team of one. We had a genuinely differentiated product, a clear ICP, and a brand voice that actually said something. But every time I opened an AI tool, I was starting over. Paste the context, explain the persona, describe the tone. Then spend an hour fixing output that sounded like every other company in the category."

"The tools weren't bad. They were just empty. No memory of what we sold, who we sold it to, or what we'd already tried. I was the repository — and I was bottlenecking everything."

"So I built a campaign engine for my own company first — a persistent repository layer that structured everything the AI needed to know, with dedicated agents for each campaign type and channel. The output quality difference was immediate and significant. What used to take four hours took twenty minutes."

"Vela is that system, productized and made available to every lean marketing team that's living the same problem. The thesis is simple: the context is the product. Generic AI fails because it's empty. Vela works because it knows your business before you brief it."

Alex Duncan
Founder, Vela · Glassboro, NJ
What we believe
The principles behind every product decision

These aren't values statements. They're the actual opinions that drive how we build.

01
Context is the product

The model isn't the moat — every competitor has access to the same foundation models. The intelligence layer built on top of your brand knowledge is what makes output actually good. We build for that layer, not the generation surface.

02
Lean teams are the most underserved buyers in marketing software

Enterprise marketing platforms are built for large teams with dedicated ops people. Solo marketers and small teams get the same complexity with none of the headcount to manage it. We build for the team of one to five doing everything themselves.

03
Generic output is worse than no output

Copy that sounds like every other company in the category actively damages brand credibility. We'd rather produce nothing than produce something that sounds like it came from a template. That standard drives every prompt architecture decision we make.

04
Your data is yours

The repository you build inside Vela belongs to you. We will never use your brand context, your campaign history, or your company data to train any shared model. This is not a feature — it's a hard line we don't cross.

05
Compounding beats one-time

A tool that gets better the longer you use it is fundamentally more valuable than one that resets every session. Everything we build is designed to compound — the repository, the feedback signals, the campaign library. Use it longer, get more from it.

06
Speed is a feature

A marketer who needs to ship a campaign today doesn't have time for a 10-step workflow. From signup to deployed campaign in under 10 minutes is not a marketing claim — it's a product constraint we design against on every feature we build.
