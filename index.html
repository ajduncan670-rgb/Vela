How it works
Brief to deployed in under 10 minutes

Three steps. Your agent is live before your next meeting ends.

01
Build your repository

Answer five plain-language questions. Upload a brand guide if you have one. Vorsa structures everything into a persistent knowledge layer your agent draws from on every generation — automatically.

02
Your agent gets configured

Vorsa builds an AI agent trained on your repository — your brand voice, your product, your buyers, your compliance rules. It knows what to say and what never to say before you write a single brief.

03
Generate and deploy

Select campaign type and channel. Brief in 30 seconds. Get a full campaign — emails, LinkedIn posts, landing page copy, sales battlecards. Push directly to Gmail or HubSpot. Done.
