Generation
The campaign engine

Eight campaign types, four channels, one agent that knows your business.

8 campaign types
Every motion you run

Product launch, lead nurture, event and webinar, sales enablement, brand awareness, re-engagement, seasonal/promo, and thought leadership. Each type triggers the right agent configuration for that specific goal.

4 channels
Format-native output

Email sequences (with subject, preview, body, and CTA per email). LinkedIn post series (with hook, body, and close per post). Landing page copy (structured by section). Sales battlecards (with objections, discovery questions, and close).
