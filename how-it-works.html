Three layers working together: a repository that knows your business, an agent that knows how to write for it, and a pipeline that takes campaigns from brief to production ready.

Step one
Build your brand repository

Most AI tools start from zero every time. Vela starts from everything you've already told it. Your repository is the persistent knowledge layer that makes every campaign contextually accurate from day one.

Answer five plain-language questions about your company, audience, and voice. Takes under 5 minutes.
Upload your brand guide, product docs, and past campaigns on Solo+. Vela extracts structured context automatically.
Set guardrails — words to never use, required disclaimers, regulatory context. Your agent respects them on every output.

Step two
Your agent gets configured automatically

Once your repository is built, Vela configures a dedicated AI agent for your account. This agent knows your brand voice, your product, your buyers, and your compliance rules before you write a single brief.

One dedicated agent per account on Free and Solo. Three configurations on Team. Custom on Enterprise.
Each agent is function-specific — an email agent knows email conventions, a LinkedIn agent knows what performs on LinkedIn.
All agents share the same repository — one source of truth for every channel.

Step three
Brief, generate, and deploy in minutes

Select your campaign type and channel. Add any specific context for this campaign. Hit Generate. Your agent produces a complete, contextually accurate campaign package — grounded in your repository, ready to deploy.

8 campaign types: product launch, lead nurture, events, thought leadership, re-engagement, and more.
4 channels: email sequences, LinkedIn posts, landing page copy, sales battlecards.
Edit inline, copy to clipboard, or push directly to Gmail. Every campaign saves to your library automatically.

The compounding advantage
Gets better every campaign you run

Every edit you make, every campaign you mark as deployed, every output you rate teaches your agent what good looks like for your brand specifically. After 90 days, the quality gap over any generic tool is significant and growing.

Day 1
Good output from your first brief

Your repository answers and voice chips give the agent enough to produce accurate, on-brand campaigns from the first generation. Better than anything you'd get from a blank ChatGPT prompt.

Day 30
Agent learning from your edits

Every inline edit you've made — changing "enterprise clients" to "bank partners," shortening calls to action, adjusting tone on specific campaign types — becomes part of your agent's institutional memory.

Day 90+
A proprietary asset that compounds

Your repository is now a unique, proprietary asset that reflects your brand's actual history, not just your onboarding answers. No competitor's tool can replicate this without starting from scratch.
